About the data and methods

Decay data: ENSDF via the IAEA LiveChart of Nuclides. Photon cross sections: NIST XCOM (Berger et al., SRD 8). (α,n): the NeuCBOT method and datasets (TALYS-1.95 cross sections and neutron spectra, SRIM stopping powers; Westerdale & Meyers, NIM A 875 (2017) 57). Beta spectra: the corrections reviewed by Hayen et al., Rev. Mod. Phys. 90 (2018) 015008, with unique-forbidden shapes and Coulomb functions after Behrens & Bühring. Each tab lists what it includes and leaves out. Source code: assets/signals-physics.js.
