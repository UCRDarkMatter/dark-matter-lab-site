About the data

  • Decay gammas, X-rays and half-lives: ENSDF, through the IAEA LiveChart of Nuclides.
  • Thermal-neutron capture (n,γ): computed from the ENSDF/CapGam level schemes used by G4RiversideCASCADE, including which lines arrive in the same cascade (coincidences and sum peaks). Elements are weighted by isotopic abundance × thermal capture cross section.
  • Inelastic scattering (n,n′γ): gamma transitions from low-lying levels of stable isotopes (ENSDF). Their strength depends on the neutron spectrum, so they are listed but not given absolute intensities.
  • Context (where sources appear, what produces them, how to test them): curated by the UCR Dark Matter and Neutrino Lab.

This is a beta. Rankings are guidance, not identifications. Found a wrong number or a missing source? Report it on GitHub.
