Dark matter limit plotter

Exclusion limits, projected sensitivities, discovery contours and model predictions for dark matter direct detection, collected from arXiv. Tick the curves you want, adjust the plot, and download the figure or the numbers.

About these curves. They were found, extracted and labeled automatically by a script that reads the figures and data files of arXiv papers. Curves marked unreviewed have not been checked by a person. The others have been checked against the original figure or come from a collaboration’s own data file, but values read from a figure can differ slightly from the published numbers, and a name, plot type or unit may still be wrong. For a publication or talk, use the collaboration’s official data release and cite the original paper.
